§ 02 — The craft

Not a model
of a dam.

A miniature dam that holds water is a nice model. A miniature dam that holds water, spins a runner and lights a bulb is a power station, and that is the only kind this channel builds.

The work happens on live watercourses, not in a tray. The stream gets blocked and turned aside so the excavation can be done dry. Footings go down to something solid. Steel is tied into the wall and the intake before a single pour, because water finds every weakness eventually and the reinforcement is the only reason the wall is still there when the reservoir is full.

Then comes the part nobody else films: the machinery. Francis runners, Archimedes screws, overshot wheels, one built from a broken bicycle rim — twenty-three different answers to the same question about blade angle, shaft alignment and clearance. The build is not finished when it looks finished. It is finished when the meter reads two hundred and twenty volts.
