§ 02 — The craft

Not a model
of a building.

Most miniature builders make something that looks finished. OUROBOROS ARQ makes something that was assembled — in the right order, out of the right materials.

The sequence is the whole point. Ground is dug and footings are formed before anything rises. Stirrups are bent and cages are tied before a column is boxed. Formwork goes up, concrete goes in wet, and the box comes off the next day. Only then does brick get laid, course by course, in running bond, with the openings formed as the wall grows rather than cut out afterwards.

It is taught in two languages at once — cimientos and foundations, mampostería and masonry, losa and slab — because the method does not change depending on who is watching. A four-minute video of a man casting a column properly has been watched fifty-four million times, and nothing in it is hurried.
