§ 02 — The craft

No montage.
No shortcuts.

The easy version of this channel is a sixty-second time-lapse. MINI ARCHITECT makes the hard version: the whole build, in one film, including the twenty minutes where somebody is just laying brick.

The bricks are real bricks, fired and sized in millimetres, and the mortar is real mortar. Nothing is glued, nothing is cast as a single piece and nothing is cut to fit. The slab is levelled, the corners go up first, a line is run between them, and then it is thousands of blocks laid one at a time in running bond — exactly the way it is done at full size, because at this scale there is no other way that holds.

What makes it unusual is the edit, or rather the absence of one. A mansion runs eighteen minutes. A villa runs thirty-four. The films hold an audience through the slow middle because the slow middle is the craft, and because by the time the roof closes you have watched every course that put it there.

“Man builds stunning mini house with small bricks.” — twenty-nine minutes, 9.3 million views
