The premise

Nine families. One chair. No good options.

On a continent where summers last years and winters can last a lifetime, nine great houses fight for control of a throne welded out of a thousand surrendered swords. It is a story about politics that keeps insisting, quietly at first, that it is really a story about the thing coming down from the north while everyone is busy stabbing each other.

The game

Alliances, marriages, poison, debt, and the slow discovery that honour is a liability in the capital. Ned Stark is the best man in the story and he is dead by episode nine.

The fire

An exiled girl sold to a warlord walks into a funeral pyre and comes out with three dragons — the first in a hundred and fifty years. Magic returns to a world that had settled comfortably into not believing in it.

The ice

Beyond a seven-hundred-foot wall of ice, something old is walking south with an army that grows every time it wins. Nobody in the south believes the reports. They are all wrong.

Drogon

black & red

Named for Khal Drogo. The largest, the wildest, the one she rode. He burned the Lannister loot train, then a city, and finally the chair itself — and carried her body east where no one could follow.

Last seen flying east, alone.

Rhaegal

green & bronze

Named for Rhaegar, the brother she never met. Jon Snow rode him, which was the first hint that dragons choose by blood and not by permission.

Shot out of the sky by Euron's scorpion off Dragonstone.

Viserion

cream & gold

Named for Viserys, the brother who sold her. Killed beyond the Wall by an ice spear, hauled from the lake by the dead, and raised with blue eyes.

Brought down the Wall. Killed by Drogon at Winterfell.

Twenty stuntmen were set alight for the Loot Train Attack — the most in a single production. The scene took four weeks to shoot.

Beyond the Wall

The Long Night

Eight thousand years ago the White Walkers came south in a winter that lasted a generation. Men built a wall, formed a watch, and slowly turned the whole thing into a story to frighten children — then into a prison for criminals, then into a joke.

The Night KingMade, not born. A man of the First Men with a dragonglass dagger in his heart, driven there by the Children of the Forest to fight the men who were burning their world.
DragonglassObsidian. Frozen fire. It shatters a Walker on contact — and there was a mountain of it under Dragonstone the entire time.
Valyrian steelForged with spells and dragonfire, and there are perhaps a few dozen blades left in the world. One of them ended the whole war in a single thrust.
55 nightsThe Long Night took eleven weeks of night shoots to film — the longest continuous battle sequence ever committed to television.

The prize

The Iron Throne

A thousand swords surrendered to Aegon the Conqueror, beaten flat and welded together in the fire of Balerion the Black Dread. Ugly, enormous and deliberately uncomfortable — Aegon believed a king should never sit easy.

The cost

Everyone who sat

Robert drank himself off it. Joffrey was poisoned beside it. Tommen stepped out of a window rather than keep it. Cersei took it over the ashes of the Great Sept and lost it in a cellar. Not one of them died in bed.

The end

And then it was slag

Drogon found her body on the steps, and did not go for the man holding the knife. He went for the chair. Whatever a dragon understands about grief, it understood exactly what had killed her.
