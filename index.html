§ 02 — The craft

Not models.
Buildings.

Most miniature builders make something that looks like a house. VFUHO makes something that behaves like one.

Every build starts underground. Footings are dug and poured. Rebar is cut, bent and tied by hand at a scale where a single steel wire is thinner than a matchstick. Only then does the first brick get laid — and from there it is thousands of them, course after course, in running bond, levelled one at a time.

The houses have plumbing that carries water, pumps that fill pools, doors that open by themselves and lights that come on behind real glass. Nothing is faked, and nothing is rushed. That is the whole discipline: the patience to build the parts nobody will ever see exactly as carefully as the parts everybody will.
